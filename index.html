The House of Obelora

We believe a good place to stay is felt before it is seen. In the hush of a lobby at first light, the weight of well made linen, a morning coffee poured exactly right.

Obelora brings that feeling to every kind of stay. Whether you arrive for a week by the sea, a night in the city, four years of university life or a quiet half hour with a book, you will find the same attention to detail and the same warmth at the door.

Our houses are designed with the character of their setting in mind, built from honest materials and staffed by people who take real pride in looking after others. Nothing is generic. Everything is considered.

01 Resorts

Retreats that follow the rhythm of the land

Our resorts are places to slow down completely. Villas open onto gardens and water, menus change with what the local farms bring in, and every day can be as full or as empty as you wish.

  • Private pool villas and garden suites
  • Spa rituals rooted in local traditions
  • Seasonal, farm to table dining
  • Guided walks, yoga at dawn and stargazing

02 Hotels

A quiet address in the middle of everything

For business, for pleasure or for both. Our city hotels pair a central location with rooms that feel like a deep breath. Thoughtful design, restful beds and a team that remembers how you take your tea.

  • Suites designed for rest and focused work
  • All day dining and an intimate evening bar
  • Meeting rooms and private event spaces
  • Personal concierge for the city

True hospitality is quiet. You notice it most in how you feel on the way home.

Our Approach

Three things we never compromise on

  1. I

    A sense of place

    Every Obelora house draws from its surroundings. Local materials, local makers and local flavours give each one a character you could not find anywhere else.

  2. II

    Craft in the details

    From the weight of a door handle to the thread count of a pillowcase, we sweat the small things so that our guests never have to think about them.

  3. III

    Care that feels personal

    Service at Obelora is warm, intuitive and never scripted. We hire for kindness, train for excellence and give our people the freedom to go further.
